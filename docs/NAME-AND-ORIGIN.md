# Why Mouse & Key Studio has a different name

Mouse & Key Studio is an independent distribution modified by **Maxim Fomin**.
It is based on [AprilNEA/OpenLogi](https://github.com/AprilNEA/OpenLogi), with
additional Windows, macro-editor, profile, tray and portable-storage changes.
It is not an official OpenLogi release and is not endorsed by its maintainers
or by Logitech. Report problems with this distribution to
[MaxITService/MouseKeyStudio](https://github.com/MaxITService/MouseKeyStudio).

## Code licenses and brand rights are separate

Upstream distributes its code under **MIT OR Apache-2.0**. These licenses permit
modified independent distributions when their conditions are followed. They do
not give ownership of upstream trademarks or brand artwork. Upstream explicitly
reserves the OpenLogi name, logo and icons and asks forks/distributions not to
use them as their own identity without prior written permission. That is why
this distribution uses a different product name, independently licensed artwork,
and separate application identifiers, settings roots, IPC endpoints, autostart
entries and MSI upgrade identities.

Internal Rust crate names, dependency coordinates, source history references,
and upstream asset-mirror labels may still say `openlogi`. They identify source
components or their actual providers; they are not the public product name.
Original copyright and license notices remain intact. New modifications are
provided under the same MIT OR Apache-2.0 choice. The binary archives include
third-party notices and the separate license for the replacement artwork.

Logitech and its product names belong to their respective owners. The new name
was chosen independently; it does not assert a registered trademark or a legal
clearance of every possible name conflict.
